## What it is

The data-visualization era that preceded the agent work: bespoke D3 dashboards
built from a shared library of hand-written render modules (tables, filters,
toplines, chart legends), fed by Python-built, pako-compressed datafeeds — plus
a 40-chart D3 prototype gallery behind them. Several are still live here:

- [NSW rent profile](/profile_nswrent.html)
- [NSW sold profile](/profile_nswsold.html)
- [Titanic profile](/profile_titanic.html)
- [Property map — Leaflet](/property_leaflet.html)
- [Property hexbin](/property_hexbin.html)
- [Calendar heatmap](/tile_calendar.html)
- [Surname culture map](/surname_culture.html)

Kept as provenance: the agent projects above sit on top of this kind of data
craft.
